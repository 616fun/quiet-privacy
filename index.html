Privacy Policy — Quiet

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Quiet is built so that there is nothing to collect. It has no account, no server, and no analytics. This policy describes that plainly.

What Quiet collects

Nothing. Quiet does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store any personal information off your device. There is no account to create, no email to provide, and no sign-in of any kind.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in Quiet is stored only on your device:

  • Session records (time, which tool you used, and any optional intensity, “did this help” answer, or private note you choose to add)
  • Thoughts you explicitly choose to keep
  • Your personalization answers (wind-down time, walk window, and similar)
  • Your reset counter

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no cloud copy and no backup we can access.

Permissions Quiet may ask for

Each is optional. Declining any of them leaves the core of the app fully working.

Health (steps) — read only. If you turn on “notice stillness,” Quiet reads your step count so it can notice when a still day has also been a loud one. Step data is read on your device, never written to, never stored off-device, and never shared with anyone. Quiet never writes anything to Health.

Apple Music. If you play music in the app, playback runs through your own Apple Music subscription. Quiet does not collect, store, or transmit anything about what you listen to.

Microphone and speech recognition. If you tap the microphone to speak a thought instead of typing it, speech is converted to text using on-device recognition only. Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Notifications. Optional check-ins are scheduled locally by your phone. There is no push server and no remote messaging.

Third parties

Quiet contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Quiet is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
